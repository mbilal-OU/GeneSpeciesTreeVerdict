# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous and welcoming research-software community. Contributions and questions are evaluated on scientific and engineering content, regardless of career stage or background.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique methods and code, not people.
- Distinguish evidence from interpretation.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and plausible alternatives.
- Share reproducible examples when reporting problems.
- Respect data confidentiality and licensing.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate scientific misrepresentation or repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, remove or lock content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.
