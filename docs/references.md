# Selected references

GeneSpeciesTreeVerdict is a diagnostic and triage layer built around established concepts in gene-tree/species-tree discordance and reconciliation. It does not replace specialist inference packages.

Key references include:

- Maddison WP. 1997. Gene Trees in Species Trees. *Systematic Biology* 46:523–536. DOI: 10.1093/sysbio/46.3.523.
- Szöllősi GJ, Tannier E, Daubin V, Boussau B. 2015. The inference of gene trees with species trees. *Systematic Biology* 64:e42–e62. DOI: 10.1093/sysbio/syu048.
- Stolzer M et al. 2012. Inferring duplications, losses, transfers and incomplete lineage sorting with nonbinary species trees. *Bioinformatics* 28:i409–i415. DOI: 10.1093/bioinformatics/bts386.
- Morel B et al. 2020. GeneRax: A Tool for Species-Tree-Aware Maximum Likelihood-Based Gene Family Tree Inference under Gene Duplication, Transfer, and Loss. *Molecular Biology and Evolution* 37:2763–2774. DOI: 10.1093/molbev/msaa141.
- Zhang C, Scornavacca C, Molloy EK, Mirarab S. 2020. ASTRAL-Pro: Quartet-Based Species-Tree Inference despite Paralogy. *Molecular Biology and Evolution* 37:3292–3307. DOI: 10.1093/molbev/msaa139.
- Morel B et al. 2024. AleRax: a tool for gene and species tree co-estimation and reconciliation under a probabilistic model of gene duplication, transfer, and loss. *Bioinformatics* 40:btae162. DOI: 10.1093/bioinformatics/btae162.

See `docs/scientific-background.md` for context and the role of specialist follow-up tools.
